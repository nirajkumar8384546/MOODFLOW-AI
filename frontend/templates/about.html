🚀 About MoodFlow AI

🌟 Who We Are

MoodFlow AI is an advanced multi-modal emotion detection platform designed to understand human emotions using AI. It analyzes facial expressions, voice tone, text input, and video signals to provide real-time emotional insights.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between technology and human emotions by providing intelligent tools that help users track, understand, and improve their emotional well-being.

⚡ What We Offer

😊 Face Emotion Detection
🎤 Voice Emotion Analysis
📝 Text Sentiment Detection
🎥 Video + Audio Fusion AI
📊 Real-time Mood Dashboard
🤖 AI Insights & Analytics

⚙️ How It Works

MoodFlow AI combines deep learning models trained on emotional datasets. It processes multiple inputs simultaneously and generates accurate predictions, which are then visualized in an interactive dashboard.

🚀 Future Scope

We aim to expand MoodFlow AI with advanced features like real-time AI assistants, mental health tracking, personalized recommendations, emotion-based music suggestions, and integration with wearable devices.
